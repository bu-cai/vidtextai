# VidText AI Pricing

VidText AI offers a free plan and a Pro plan (coming soon).

## Free Plan — $0/forever

- 3 AI content generations per day (resets midnight UTC)
- Full YouTube transcript extraction (unlimited, always free)
- All 5 output formats: Transcript, Summary, Blog Post, Study Notes, Shorts Script
- 10 output languages
- Copy and download results
- No sign-up or credit card required

## Pro Plan — $9/month (Coming Soon)

- Unlimited AI content generations
- Priority processing speed
- All 10 output languages
- Custom AI prompts
- Batch processing (coming soon)
- API access (coming soon)
- Priority support
- No ads

## What Counts as One AI Generation?

Each time you generate a Summary, Blog Post, Study Notes, or Shorts Script uses one generation.
Fetching a plain transcript does NOT count toward your daily limit.
